# Owner notice: ZexCore online payment fees

**Subject:** Important update: fees for online payments on ZexCore

Hello,

We are making online payments available across every ZexCore plan. To support payment processing and the platform, ZexCore applies a fee to successful online booking deposits and shop orders.

The fee is based on your workspace's effective plan:

| Plan | ZexCore fee |
|---|---:|
| Free | 2% |
| Starter | 1.5% |
| Growth | 1% |
| Agency | 0.5% |
| Enterprise | 0.5% |
| Dedicated | 0.5% |

**How this affects you**

- The ZexCore fee is deducted from your business settlement; it is not added to the amount your customer sees or pays at checkout.
- Paystack processing fees are separate and are also borne by the business.
- The applicable ZexCore rate is determined by your effective plan when the payment is started.
- Each payment record keeps its fee amount and plan for reconciliation.

Please sign in to ZexCore and open **Settings → Online payment fees** to review the full terms. The workspace owner must explicitly accept the current fee policy before new online deposits and shop checkouts can be started.

If you do not wish to accept these terms, you can continue arranging payment directly with customers instead of using ZexCore online checkout.

Thank you for building with ZexCore.

**The ZexCore Team**
